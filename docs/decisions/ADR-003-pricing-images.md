# ADR-003 — Pricing & Images

## Decision
Pricing belongs to the vehicle model by default. V1 does not expose physical-car price overrides.

Generic model images are stored once at model level. Optional actual vehicle photos attach to physical vehicles. The public gallery may combine both.

## Reason
Owners normally rent all units of the same model at the same rate and should not have to maintain per-car prices.
Customers should see real business fleet imagery without being forced to choose the exact physical unit.
