# Rental Platform V1 — Product Overview

Status: FROZEN PLANNING REQUIREMENT
Target: small/local rental businesses, starting with Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu
Primary vertical: car/self-drive rental
Future extensibility: bike rental, equipment rental, event-equipment rental

## Product goal
Provide a professional public business website plus a lightweight owner dashboard that manages rental requests, physical fleet availability and booking operations.

## Core product promise
The product is a business solution, not merely a brochure website. It should reduce repetitive booking/availability work while keeping the owner in control of real-world decisions.

## Core principles
1. Owner action is authoritative for booking confirmation.
2. Pending requests do not consume inventory.
3. Availability is calculated by the system from the current fleet state, confirmed bookings, vehicle blocks and business closures.
4. Customer login/account is not required.
5. The public website and owner dashboard are one responsive web application.
6. Keep V1 intentionally small and practical for local businesses.
7. Do not silently invent features, business rules or policy enforcement not specified here.
8. Do not expose internal physical-vehicle identifiers in the normal customer journey.
9. Client-owned production accounts are preferred.

## V1 non-goals
- Online payments
- KYC / ID uploads or verification
- Digital rental agreements
- Accounting / profit / loss
- Partner settlement or revenue-share calculations
- Vehicle maintenance module
- GPS/telematics
- Automated penalty calculation
- Customer accounts
- Employee/staff roles in V1
- Native mobile application
- Advanced pricing engine
- Multi-branch operations
- Full CRM
- In-app messaging inbox
- Guaranteed SEO rankings
- Advanced analytics platform
